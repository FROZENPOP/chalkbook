CLIMB LOG

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

Your climbing journal stays on your iPhone. Nothing leaves your device unless you join Friends and choose to share a session. We don’t sell data, show ads, or track you across apps or websites.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Your sessions, climbs, attempts, notes, gyms, photos and videos.
  • Apple Health data. If you turn on Health insights, Climb Log reads the workouts, energy, heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability you allow. It is used only on your device, is never sent to our servers, and is never used for advertising.
  • Backups you export. They are files you control.

Signing in

You can use Climb Log without an account. If you sign in with Google or Apple, your name, email and account identifier are kept in your iPhone’s Keychain so the app can keep you signed in.

Friends (optional)

If you join Friends, we store the following with our backend provider, Supabase, so other climbers can find you and see what you share:

  • Your account identifier and the email address from your sign-in provider. Your email is never shown to other climbers.
  • Your username, display name, and optional bio and profile photo.
  • Friend requests, friendships, blocks and reports.
  • Sessions you choose to share: date, caption, attempts, sends, duration and any route photos you attach.
  • Reactions you leave on friends’ sessions.

Private profiles are visible only to approved friends. Shared sessions are visible only to the audience you choose.

Deleting your data

  • Delete account (Settings) permanently deletes your account, Friends profile, connections, shared sessions and reactions from our servers.
  • Delete social profile (Friends) removes your Friends presence but keeps you signed in.
  • Deleting a session in the app also removes it from Friends.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone.

Community safety

You can report or block any climber. We review reports and remove content and accounts that break the community rules.

Children

Climb Log is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
